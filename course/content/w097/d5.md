## What they're asking

Behind each question is a **competency**: prioritising, handling feedback, communication, learning from failure, working with users, leadership. Interviewers want evidence from the past ("tell me about a time"), not promises ("I would..."). They listen for what **you** did (not "we"), how you **thought** about it, and what **changed** as a result. They also notice honesty: a failure story where you admit the mistake and show what you learned is stronger than a failure that was really someone else's fault.

## STAR

A reliable structure, kept to about two minutes (300 words at a speaking pace):

- **Situation**: a sentence or two of context.
- **Task**: what needed to happen, and your part in it.
- **Action**: what you did, step by step, and why. This is most of the answer.
- **Result**: what happened, with a number if you can (a time saved, a score that rose, a release that shipped), and what you learned.

Say "I" for your own actions. Cut hedging ("I think maybe", "sort of", "I guess"): it makes good work sound accidental.

## A story bank

Prepare six to eight stories, each tagged with the competencies it shows, and practise them aloud. Most questions can be answered from a good bank: the sprint where you cut export answers "prioritising", "deadlines" and "communication". Spread the stories, so one interview doesn't hear the same one three times. And expect **follow-ups** ("why did you choose that?", "what would you do differently?"): a story with a clear reason and a lesson already answers them.

import re

PARTS = ["situation", "task", "action", "result"]

def star_problems(story):
    problems = [f"missing {p}" for p in PARTS if not story.get(p, "").strip()]
    words = {p: len(story.get(p, "").split()) for p in PARTS}
    if words["action"] < words["situation"] or words["action"] < 25:
        problems.append("say more about what you did")
    action = story.get("action", "").lower()
    if len(re.findall(r"\bwe\b", action)) > len(re.findall(r"\bi\b", action)):
        problems.append("say what you did, not only the team")
    if not re.search(r"\d", story.get("result", "")):
        problems.append("quantify the result")
    if sum(words.values()) > 300:
        problems.append("too long for two minutes")
    return problems

def match_stories(stories, questions):
    used = {s["id"]: 0 for s in stories}
    matches, unmatched = [], []
    for text, competency in questions:
        options = [s for s in stories if competency in s["tags"]]
        if not options:
            unmatched.append(text)
            continue
        best = min(options, key=lambda s: used[s["id"]])
        used[best["id"]] += 1
        matches.append((text, best["id"]))
    return matches, unmatched

PHRASES = ["i think maybe", "sort of", "kind of", "i guess", "probably", "just", "a bit"]

def hedges(text):
    lower = text.lower()
    return [p for p in PHRASES if re.search(rf"\b{re.escape(p)}\b", lower)]

    matches, unmatched = match_stories(stories, questions)
    lines, weak = [], []
    for text, sid in matches:

        whole = " ".join(story[p] for p in ("situation", "task", "action", "result"))
        lines.append(f"{text} -> {sid} ({speaking_time(whole)})")
